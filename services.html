Questions

Before you book.

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings we suggest three to four months ahead. Portrait and landscape sessions can usually be scheduled within two weeks.

Do you travel outside Salem?

Yes. We shoot across Tamil Nadu and Karnataka, and travel further for weddings and photo trips. Travel costs are quoted up front.

When will we receive our photographs?

A highlight gallery arrives within seven days and the full edited set within two weeks of the shoot.

Can we buy prints and albums?

Every package can include fine-art prints, framed pieces or a hand-bound album, printed on archival paper.

What happens if it rains?

We keep a backup slot for every outdoor shoot, and monsoon light often makes for the most dramatic frames.

Not sure which shoot fits?

Tell us what you have in mind and we will suggest the right plan, no pressure.
